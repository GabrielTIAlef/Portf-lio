01 / Context

The setting and the scope.

An unsupervised learning study evolved from a notebook PDF into an executable implementation. The public workflow uses fictional customers and is distinguished from the historical marketing dashboard.

02 / Problem & objective

What needed to be solved.

Clustering requires explaining the features, the number of groups and the limitations. The analysis needed alternative comparisons, reconciled profiles and a reproducible workflow.

03 / Architecture & development

From source to delivery.

  1. 01Fictional / authorized CSV
  2. 02Contract + scaling
  3. 03KMeans + evaluation
  4. 04Profiles + plots

A simplified reading guide. Repository documentation describes contracts, sources, stages and reproduction.

04 / Challenges & decisions

Decisions behind the solution.

  • StandardScaler with explicit seeds and initialization settings.
  • Compare k from 2 to 6 using silhouette, inertia and group sizes.
  • Assess seed stability with ARI and sensitivity to RobustScaler.
  • The notebook uses the tested modules; generation manifests and hashes preserve valid output.

05 / Results & evidence

What can be reviewed.

The 180-customer demonstration selected three groups of 60 with a silhouette score of about 0.727. Components were designed into the generator: this validates the workflow, rather than discovering natural segments in real customers.

Public evidence

Public notebook, profiles, k comparisons and plots; documentation records 43 tests. Main-branch CI was successful when checked on 21 October 2026.

06 / Limitations

The boundaries of this work.

This is exploratory segmentation, not a supervised or production-deployed model. An artificial spending score does not measure loyalty, purchase frequency or conversion. Campaign applications remain hypotheses.
