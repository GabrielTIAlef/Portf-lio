01 / Context

The setting and the scope

At IZZI, tasks, client records and manual controls were spread across tools. My work connected Gestta and Notion to PostgreSQL and Power BI for team, deadline and client onboarding reporting. Operational, service and processing dashboards supported analysis of tasks, requests and pending stages.

02 / Problem & objective

What needed to be solved

Slow updates and spreadsheet dependency made delays and bottlenecks harder to identify. Tasks and clients needed to be linked without dropping unmatched records, creating a consistent foundation for reporting.

03 / Architecture & development

From source to delivery

  1. 01Gestta / Notion
  2. 02Python + validation
  3. 03PostgreSQL + views
  4. 04Files + manifest

A simplified reading guide. Repository documentation describes contracts, sources, stages and reproduction.

04 / Challenges & decisions

Decisions behind the solution

  • The pipeline coordinates extraction, validation, transformation and loading within the same run.
  • CNPJ identifiers remain text; extraction failures and invalid contracts stop publication.
  • PostgreSQL transactions and locks protect tables and views; a hash manifest identifies confirmed files.
  • Power BI refresh is optional and requests an update without confirming its completion.

05 / Results & evidence

What can be reviewed

In the operation addressed, updates went from five days to under five minutes according to the documented professional account, centralizing sources and reducing reliance on manual controls. The public version makes the design inspectable through five fictional tasks, two clients and four checklist items, including an unmatched task.

Public evidence

Pipeline, transformations, views and a disposable PostgreSQL demonstration. Documentation records 72 tests, including 14 using real PostgreSQL.
