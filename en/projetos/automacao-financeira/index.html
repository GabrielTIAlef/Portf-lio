01 / Context

The setting and the scope

The original professional project connected Conta Azul, Notion, Excel and PostgreSQL for financial monitoring. The new public version supports technical review without corporate accounts or files.

02 / Problem & objective

What needed to be solved

External failures, similar names and concurrent file changes require explicit identity, retry and synchronization rules, without assuming every system updates together.

03 / Architecture & development

From source to delivery

  1. 01Conta Azul / Notion
  2. 02UUID contract
  3. 03PostgreSQL + outbox
  4. 04Excel / synchronization

A simplified reading guide. Repository documentation describes contracts, sources, stages and reproduction.

04 / Challenges & decisions

Decisions behind the solution

  • UUIDs identify customers, avoiding name-based matching.
  • Snapshots, batch records and delivery intents share one PostgreSQL transaction.
  • An outbox records delivery states and avoids blindly repeating ambiguous remote creates.
  • A serial worker, debounce and polling manage file changes and refresh requests.

05 / Results & evidence

What can be reviewed

The demo combines three fictional customers, two receivables and simulated services with a disposable real database. It demonstrates contracts, reconciliation and recovery, without measuring business impact or time savings.

Public evidence

Code, DDL, a demo and documentation of 65 tests, including 24 with PostgreSQL. Main-branch CI was successful when checked on 21 October 2026.
